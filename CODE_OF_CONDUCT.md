# Code of Conduct

Mode is built by people from many places and backgrounds. We want taking part to be a
good experience for everyone, whatever their age, body, ability, ethnicity, gender
identity or expression, experience, nationality, appearance, race, religion or sexual
identity and orientation.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new.
- Assume good intent, and disagree about ideas, not people.
- Give feedback that helps, and take it gracefully.
- Credit others’ work.

## What isn’t acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexual language or imagery, or unwanted attention.
- Publishing anyone’s private information without their permission.
- Anything else that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in every Mode space: issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews, and
when you represent the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

If someone’s behaviour worries you, contact the maintainer privately through
[GitHub](https://github.com/Piyusinha). Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may
remove comments, close threads or ban anyone who breaks this code, with a warning first
where that makes sense.

This code is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org),
version 2.1.
